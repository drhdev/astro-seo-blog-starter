# SEO Audit Prompt

Audit the website for technical SEO.

Check:

- unique titles and descriptions
- canonicals
- Open Graph images
- Twitter card metadata
- JSON-LD validity
- sitemap
- RSS
- robots.txt
- featured images and alt text
- internal links
- noindex placeholders
- draft and future post filtering

Do not make risky content claims. Fix safe technical issues and summarize remaining manual checks.
